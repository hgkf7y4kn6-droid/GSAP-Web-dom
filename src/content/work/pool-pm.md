## The problem

A pool build runs from the first lead through design, permits, construction and handover, then into years of warranty work. Builders often track all of that across spreadsheets, texts and paper, and much of the work happens on job sites where the signal is unreliable.

## What I built

Pool PM is a project management app for pool builders that follows a job from lead to warranty in one place. It's built offline-first, so the app is designed to keep working on a job site without a reliable connection.

The mobile app is built with Expo and React Native. It talks to a Fastify API backed by PostgreSQL, with Redis alongside it.
