## The problem

Subscriptions renew quietly and spending drifts across several accounts. Most people only notice when a statement surprises them.

## What I built

KaventaFi is a personal finance app for tracking subscriptions and spending insights, including AI-generated insights. Bank accounts are connected through Plaid, so the app works from real transactions rather than manual entry.

The app is built with Expo and React Native, with a FastAPI backend that handles the Plaid connection.

## Where it stands

KaventaFi is live and still in progress.
