## The problem

Weekly daily fantasy sports (DFS) lineup decisions often come down to gut feel and data scattered across several sites.

## What I built

Riches Analytics is a live sports analytics site that runs Monte Carlo simulations on NFL games. It sets those simulations alongside real DraftKings salaries and Sleeper projections, so lineup decisions can be weighed against simulated outcomes rather than a single projection.

It's built in Python with FastAPI.

## Where it stands

Riches Analytics is live, and it's the most polished of my projects so far.
