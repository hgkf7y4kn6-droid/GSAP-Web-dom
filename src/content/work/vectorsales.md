## The problem

Shoppers rarely have an easy way to compare products on what they really cost over their life. The sticker price hides the cost per use.

## What I built

VectorSales matches products into good, better and best tiers and compares the options on cost per use. It pairs CLIP image embeddings with FAISS similarity search to find comparable products, and uses OCR to read product details.

The backend is FastAPI, deployed on Render and Cloudflare.

## Where it stands

VectorSales is deployed and still being developed. The code is on GitHub.
